RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) combines retrieved source passages with a language model to ground an answer in relevant material. Retrieval happens before generation, and the answer should cite or reflect the retrieved evidence.

An AI agent can plan actions, use tools, observe results, and verify or revise its work. A plain chatbot mainly produces text in a turn without necessarily acting or checking.

A guardrail validates input or output and stops unsafe or unreliable behavior. Human-in-the-loop review means a person evaluates or approves an important AI result.

Reliability testing measures whether an AI system is consistent, accurate, and robust. Outputs should be checked through verification, which compares an answer against requirements or source evidence.
